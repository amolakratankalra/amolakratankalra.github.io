Hi! I am Amolak Ratan Kalra. I am interested in the mathematics of quantum information. Some topics I am interested in are:

  1. Mathematical Theory of Quantum Circuits
  2. Quantum Error Correction and Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing
  3. Quantum Shannon Theory
  4. Non-locality, Magic and Contextuality

Although the overarching goal of my research program is to get a deeper theoretical understanding of the power and limitations of quantum information, many of these themes also have significant practical implications.
